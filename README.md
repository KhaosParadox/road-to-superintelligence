# road-to-superintelligence

I'm a cognitive ergonomics student who wants to have fun building AI, trying to undestand what's in the black box.
Step by step, I will build an AI named Omi (short for "Other mind") and explore its abilities.

Here are the steps: 

|Arc|What happens|Status|
|---|-------------------|-------|
|0 |Setting up the lab|done|
|1 |Omi will learn to distinguish between different shapes||
|2|Omi will be trained on chess PGNs first, then learn to talk, and finally be fine-tuned to have its own way of speaking||
|3 |I will do some cognitive experiments on Omi and compare them to human data||
|4 |I will try to understand what's in Omi's mind (interpretability work)||
|5 |I will check whether Omi is loyal and aligned||
|6 |I will give Omi a body (or at least a hand)||
|7 |Omi will play ARC-AGI-3||
|8 |Omi will self-improve||
